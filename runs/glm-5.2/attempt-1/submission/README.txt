SERAPHIM KEYGEN  -  original FastTracker II keygen tune
======================================================
12 channels / 13 instruments / 11 pattern positions, all samples and
patterns generated from scratch (see src/).

Style ..... classic 90s keygen / cracktro chiptune
Key ....... D minor (i - VI - III - VII family harmony)
Tempo ..... 150 BPM, 1 row = 1/16th, speed 6
Length .... 70.4 s first pass, loops from position 1 (64 s loop)

Arrangement (each pattern = 4 bars):
  pos  0  P0  intro: drums + arp build, bass/pad enter
  pos  1  P1  verse groove            <- song loop restarts here
  pos  2  P2  verse + lead melody A
  pos  3  P3  verse + lead melody A2, 16th arpeggios, fill
  pos  4  P4  chorus 1 (crash, full kit, pad, lead hook, bell sparkle)
  pos  5  P5  chorus 2, rising scale run, fill
  pos  6  P6  break: half-time kit, swelling pad chords, bell music box
  pos  7  P7  build: snare roll 8ths -> 16ths, 16th bass, noise riser
  pos  8  P9  chorus 1 again, now with harmonised lead doubling
  pos  9  P10 chorus 2 again + harmony, rising run
  pos 10  P8  turn: melody resolves, machine-gun stutter roll -> loop

Instruments: kick, snare, closed/open hat, pluck bass (sweeping
harmonic cutoff), pulse arp, 3x detuned saw pad (L/C/R), saw lead with
baked 5.5 Hz vibrato, FM bell, noise riser, lead-harmony copy.
All pitched samples are tuned so that their base note plays at 44.1 kHz;
pad and lead use seamless 2 s loops (all partials are exact multiples of
0.5 Hz) and are released with volume-column fades + note-offs.

preview.wav is a straight render of the module (one full pass, 44.1 kHz).
