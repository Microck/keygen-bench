Neon Keygen — original keygen tune (XM, 12ch, 10 instruments)

- Format: FastTracker II XM, 12 channels, speed 3 / BPM 148 (row ~50.7ms, pattern 64 rows ~3.24s)
- Length: 16 orders (12 unique patterns), ~51.8s per loop, restart at 0, loops cleanly (loop-seam OFFs, ends at digital 0)
- Harmony: C major / A minor. Am - F - G - C anthem core; verses C / F-G; bridge Em-F-G; outro F-G walk back to C.
- Instruments (all synthesized here with NumPy, no external material):
  1 SqrLead  (detuned square-ish, looped, C4-recorded)
  2 SawBass  (12-harmonic saw + sub, looped)
  3 ArpPlk   (one-shot decaying pluck)
  4 FifthPad (C+G detuned power-chord pad, looped)
  5 Kick, 6 Snare, 7 CHH, 8 OHH, 9 Crash, 10 Sweep (one-shots)
- Arrangement: intro (pad+pluck) -> teaser -> verse -> chorus -> anthem -> half-time break -> anthem -> snare-roll build -> peak octave lead -> verse2 -> bridge -> anthem -> break -> outro (resolves, note-OFFs for seamless loop)
- Mix: peak ~-0.1dBFS, zero full-scale clips, RMS ~-15dBFS; slight stereo (hats L, arp/ohh R, rest center, mono-safe).
- Files: tune.xm (final module), preview.wav (tracker render), scripts in /workspace/scripts reproduce samples+composition.

Build: scripts/make_samples.py + make_loops_fixed.py (samples), compose.py (notes), remix.py/vol/fixseam/offs/fixend/stereo (mix + loop seam).
