Neon Keygen - original keygen tune
==================================
Format: FastTracker II XM, 12 channels, 8 patterns x 64 rows
Tempo: 150 BPM, speed 6, 4/4, 51.2s loop
Key: A minor, progression Am-F-C-G throughout

Channels:
 0 Lead (square, vibrato)
 1 Echo (lead delayed 3 rows)
 2 Arp (16th arpeggio)
 3 Bass (8th driving)
 4 Kick (4-on-floor)
 5 Snare (2 & 4 + fills)
 6 Hat closed
 7 Hat open / crash
 8-10 Pad triad (root-third-fifth)
 11 Sweep riser + pluck stabs

Instruments: 10 original synthesized samples (44100Hz 16-bit mono)
  lead, arp, bass, pad (looped, forward loop 16-bit)
  kick, snare, hats, pluck, sweep (one-shot)
All samples synthesized with Python/NumPy, tuned to C-4.
Volumes via volume column (V+16), panning via sample panning.
Loops cleanly from end to start (loop_start 0).

Files:
 tune.xm - final module
 preview.wav - render at 44100/16-bit
 make_samples.py - sample synthesis
 generate.py - composition (patterns/order)
 lead_data.json - lead melody data
