"synthetic dreams" - original keygen tune (FastTracker II module)

  tune.xm      12 channels, 14 instruments, 12 patterns of 64 rows,
               152 BPM / speed 6, restart position 2 (seamless loop).
  preview.wav  render of one pass (75.8 s) made with module_render.
  synth.py     NumPy synthesis of every sample (kick, snare, clap, hats,
               crash, riser, pluck bass, chip arp L/R, detuned pulse lead,
               delayed lead copy, fifth-stacked pad, bell).
  compose.py   builds the patterns / order and emits the FT2 tool calls.

Key A minor, 152 BPM.  Form: intro (0-1) -> A theme (2-3) -> B theme (4-5)
-> break (6) -> build (7) -> A reprise with third harmony + dotted-8th echo
(8-9) -> B reprise with octave double (10-11) -> loops back to pattern 2.
Chords: Am F C G (A section) and Dm Am F E (B section, harmonic-minor
cadence).  No envelopes were available, so every attack/decay is baked into
the sample data; note tails are faded with Axx volume slides.
