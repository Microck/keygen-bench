# Digital Sunrise - Original Keygen Tune

## Overview
An original keygen-style electronic music composition created with FastTracker II.

## Technical Details
- **Format**: Extended Module (XM)
- **Channels**: 8
- **Tempo**: 148 BPM
- **Speed**: 3 ticks per row
- **Duration**: ~26 seconds per loop
- **Key**: A minor

## Structure
- **Pattern 0**: Intro - Drums building up, bass enters halfway
- **Pattern 1**: Section A - Full arrangement with catchy lead melody
- **Pattern 2**: Section B - Arpeggio section with driving bass
- **Pattern 3**: Break - Atmospheric pad with soft lead
- **Pattern 4**: Climax - Full throttle with all elements
- **Pattern 5**: Outro - Wind down
- **Pattern 6**: Section A variation - Different melody
- **Pattern 7**: Transition fill - Drum fill leading back

**Order**: 0 → 1 → 2 → 6 → 3 → 4 → 7 → 2 → (loops back to 1)

## Instruments (All Original Samples)
1. **Kick** - Punchy synthesized kick drum with pitch envelope
2. **Snare** - Sharp snare with noise and tone blend
3. **Hi-Hat** - Crisp closed hi-hat
4. **Open Hat** - Sustained open hi-hat
5. **Bass** - Square wave bass with filter envelope
6. **Lead** - Bright saw-wave lead synth with detuning
7. **Arp** - Plucky arpeggio synth
8. **Pad** - Warm detuned pad for atmosphere
9. **Crash** - Noise-based crash cymbal

## Musical Elements
- **Chord Progression**: Am - F - C - E (i - VI - III - VII)
- **Bass Line**: Active 8th notes following chord roots
- **Melody**: Catchy, memorable phrases in A minor
- **Arpeggios**: Fast 16th-note patterns adding energy
- **Drums**: Driving beat with kicks on 1 & 3, snares on 2 & 4

## Files
- `tune.xm` - The complete tracker module (editable)
- `preview.wav` - Rendered audio preview (single loop)
- `loop_test.wav` - Rendered with loop flag set

## Loop Behavior
The module is designed to loop cleanly from the end back to position 1 (Section A), creating a seamless循环 for extended playback.
