Digital Cascade - Original Keygen Tune
=======================================

Composition Details:
- Format: FastTracker II Extended Module (XM)
- Channels: 8
- BPM: 140
- Speed: 3
- Duration: ~34 seconds (loops)
- Key: A minor

Structure:
- Intro (sparse build-up)
- Main Section A (full arrangement with lead melody)
- Main Section B (variation with reversed arpeggios)
- Break (minimal, atmospheric)
- Main Section A Reprise (with fills)
- Outro (wind down)
- Loops back to Main Section A

Instruments:
1. Kick - Synthesized punchy kick drum
2. Snare - Noise burst with tonal component
3. Hi-Hat - Short noise burst
4. Open Hat - Longer decay hat
5. Bass - Fat sawtooth with sub bass
6. Lead - Detuned saw/square wave synth
7. Arp - Bright plucky arpeggio synth
8. Pad - Warm detuned pad

Chord Progression: Am - F - C - E
(i - VI - III - V in A minor)

The module loops cleanly from the end back to position 1 (after the intro).
