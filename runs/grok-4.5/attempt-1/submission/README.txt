Keygen Crystal v1
=================
Original keygen-style module composed in FT2-compatible XM format.

Tempo: 140 BPM, speed 6
Channels: 8
Length: 16 order positions, loops from position 2 (after intro)
Progression A: Am - F - C - G
Progression B: Dm - Am - F - E

Instruments: kick, snare, hats, bass, lead saw, pad, pluck arp,
chord stab, tom, square bleep, sub, noise riser.

Arrangement:
 0 intro pads/arp
 1 drums enter
 2-3 full groove A + lead hook
 4-5 groove B + ascending lead
 6 break
 7 build
 2-5 reprise
 8 climax
 9 cool-down
 4-5 tag into loop
